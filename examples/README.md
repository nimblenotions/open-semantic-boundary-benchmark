# Examples

The CIKM 2026 evaluation is the synthetic medication-adherence study in [`medication_adherence/`](medication_adherence/README.md). The other folders are small illustrations: they help you recognize a similar disclosure problem and inspect what an export and provenance record look like. They do not contribute additional benchmark results.

| Status | Folder |
|--------|--------|
| **CIKM 2026 study** | [`medication_adherence/`](medication_adherence/README.md) |
| **Format examples** | [`provenance/`](provenance/README.md) |
| **Pilot record shape** | [`bring_your_own/`](bring_your_own/README.md) |
| **Conceptual domains; not evaluated** | [`agent_observability/`](agent_observability/README.md), [`customer_support/`](customer_support/README.md), [`financial_chatbot/`](financial_chatbot/README.md) |

For the published protocol and frozen results, start with the [root README](../README.md) and [`../releases/cikm-2026/`](../releases/cikm-2026/).
